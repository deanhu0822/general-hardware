# Caid Labs — J12 Review Packet

Updated 24 October 2026. This package specifies the narrowed hackathon build. It is not evidence of a deployed application, real revenue or completed participant research.

## The business

**Send a connector-change request and supporting documents. Receive a source-linked engineering review packet showing alternatives, affected artifacts, missing evidence and the next decision.**

The first customer is a forward-deployed engineer or small robotics team preparing a J12 connector-substitution review. The paid output is preparation of a review packet. It is not certification, a completed test or permission to release hardware.

The complete business loop is: **intake → Whop purchase → bounded agent work → packet delivery → customer clarification → measured Terac feedback → product improvement**.

One offer, one scenario, one delivery artifact. Keep the eight sector tags as metadata; demonstrate only Robotics & Industrial. Defer MiroFish, CAD/3D, live product graphs, additional source connectors, sector-specific workflows, recurring billing and a general project-management suite.
